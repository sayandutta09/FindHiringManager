FAQ

What happens in the free preview?

Your browser reads the pasted text and prepares the likely contact categories around the role. It does not send the job description to Gemini or search the web.

Does the preview find real people?

No. Names, current roles, source links, and relevance require the paid contact search. Checkout and paid search are still being connected.

When will a credit be used?

A credit will be used only after an authenticated user requests a real contact search. Previewing the role will remain free.

Are paid results guaranteed to be correct?

No. Results will use public web information and can be wrong or outdated. Every person and source should be verified before outreach.
